# Customer policies

Public routes: /privacy-policy, /shipping-policy, /refund-policy and /terms-and-conditions.

The shared wording in lib/store-policies.mjs replaces the old demo descriptions. Public pages display the bundled policies immediately when no usable published database version is available. Published administrator edits override the bundle, except known obsolete demo or test-fixture copy. The pages show an updated date, section navigation, related support links and the known customer care number.

At /admin/content, an owner or administrator can use Publish updated policies to save the same four documents through the existing role checked dl_admin_write function. This intentionally replaces the existing text of those four policy records. It leaves other content and checkout settings unchanged. Database saves also satisfy the existing published-policy prerequisite for checkout; stock, coverage, tax and payment configuration are separate. If a database write fails midway, the action reports the count saved and permits an idempotent retry. No extra SQL migration is needed beyond the existing live-commerce migration.

The copy is original and tailored to the actual platform. It covers essential cookies, Supabase authentication, Razorpay processing, Resend transactional messages, WhatsApp drafts, the browser-based platform guide, retention, access and correction requests, fresh produce exceptions, quality claims, cancellation, approved refunds, deliveries, subscription schedules and complimentary trays. It does not create a recurring payment mandate, a universal delivery guarantee, a fixed bank processing promise or a waiver of mandatory consumer remedies. It does not require an unboxing video for a genuine claim.

The legal seller's verified name, registered business address, and designated grievance officer/contact were not supplied. They have not been invented. Add these verified disclosures to the relevant published policies and customer contact details before launch. No seller identity, registration, FSSAI or GST number or business support email is inferred from an administrator login address. No claim of comprehensive legal compliance or legal review is made.
