# Detoxleaf brand direction

Reference: brand-reference.png, supplied by the user.

Name: Detoxleaf. Tagline: Pure Wellness From Nature.
Palette: Forest Green #1B5E20, Leaf Green #7CB342, Fresh Green #C5E1A5, Earth Brown #8D6E63, Cream #F9F7EF, Charcoal #263238.
Typography: Poppins primary; DM Sans secondary. Maintain the user's request for readable text, premium design, and a natural grass video hero.
Voice: simple, warm, nature-first, educational and trustworthy. Product remains fresh wheatgrass trays; powder packaging in the reference is inspiration, not an existing SKU.
Logo: preserve colours, proportions and clear space; light background preferred. The reference board is archived; obtain an original standalone logo asset before replacing the current wordmark. Do not recreate the detailed logo inaccurately from the low-resolution board.
Claims: use accurate, supportable language. Reference health slogans are creative examples, not substantiation for medical, immunity, detoxification or disease claims. Do not publish unverified claims or customer results.
Media: natural, uncluttered imagery, real footage, gentle colour treatment; avoid heavy filters. Social formats and campaign examples in the board guide future assets rather than website layout dimensions.

Standalone logo and wheatgrass powder packaging supplied by the user are now stored in public/brand. The powder mockup is shown separately as a coming-soon product; no powder pricing or health claims have been invented.

Motion: framer-motion scroll reveals, staggered farm/process and subscription cards, and animated cart entrance/exit. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep server-rendered content visible before JavaScript loads.

Shared gradient system: app/design-tokens.css defines full botanical, deep high-contrast, soft botanical and glow tokens. Full gradient is reserved for the farm section; deep gradient is used on About's philosophy band, announcement, footer and primary buttons; soft gradient supports product imagery, plan and recipe cards. Reuse these tokens for new pages. Reading and primary product areas remain cream.
